# Meta Snapshot — April 10, 2026 (Day 2)
*Source: CybertronVGC YouTube analysis + Pikalytics championspreview data*

## Meta Summary

Format is shifting fast. Day 1 was weather-dominated (Pelipper/Archaludon, Charizard Y/Venusaur/Whimsicott sun). Day 2+ is seeing major Japanese innovations.

## Top Megas (by impact)

| Mega | Status | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Floette** | Broken | Calm Mind + Moonblast one-shots the format. Fairy Aura. Most teams are "run Floette or beat Floette." |
| **Charizard Y** | Strong | Sun + Heatwave + Solar Beam. Counters Floette teams. Walled by Garchomp. |
| **Gengar** | Strong | Shadow Tag + Perish Song. Sludge Bomb does 80% to bulky Floette. |
| **Delphox** | Rising fast | Won biggest tournament. Levitate lets it pair with EQ users. Heatwave pressure. Top players going 22-1 with it. |
| **Kangaskhan** | Fraud | Power Up Punch removed. Hard-countered by the top 3 non-megas (Incineroar, Sinistcha, Sneasler). |
| **Kingambit** | Solid | Often paired with Sneasler. Dark/Steel but doesn't wall Floette well (Iron Head doesn't KO). |
| **Gardevoir** | Budget Floette | 80% of a Floette. Good substitute if you don't have Floette. |

## Top Non-Megas

The "big 3" found on almost every team:
1. **Incineroar** — Fake Out + Intimidate + Parting Shot
2. **Sinistcha** — Rage Powder + Hospitality + Trick Room. Walls Kangaskhan completely.
3. **Sneasler** — Fast Fake Out + Close Combat. Best Kingambit counter.

## Key Trends

### Earthquake + Flying/Levitate is the dominant strategy
- Garchomp + Aerodactyl (Tailwind + EQ, then Rock Slide + EQ spam)
- Garchomp + Talonflame (EQ + Tailwind, then EQ + Brave Bird/Flare Blitz)
- Garchomp + Delphox (Levitate, EQ + Heatwave)
- Garchomp/Excadrill + Rotom-Wash/Heat (Levitate, EQ + Discharge combo)

### Rising Pokemon
- **Aerodactyl** — Fastest mon in format, Tailwind, Dual Wing Beat breaks Sneasler sash, Rock Slide STAB kills Charizard. Everywhere on top Japanese teams.
- **Garchomp** — Walls Charizard Y. Earthquake + Stomping Tantrum. Found on balance and Floette teams.
- **Aegislash** — Rising as one of few quality Steels that wall Floette.
- **Maushold** — Friend Guard for Floette, Super Fang, Follow Me, Taunt. Replacing Sinistcha on some Japanese teams.
- **Talonflame** — Tailwind + Flying + Will-O-Wisp. Pairs with Garchomp.
- **Rotom-Wash/Heat** — Will-O-Wisp cripples physical meta, Levitate for EQ immunity, Volt Switch pivoting.
- **Kommo-o** — Beast if opponent lacks Fairy coverage. Weather hate (beats sand and sun cores).
