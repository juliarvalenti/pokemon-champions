# Alakazam Screens Team (Planned)

*Bulky offense / screens HO archetype. Alakazam sets Light Screen + Reflect turn 1, then Speed Swaps onto a slow but monstrous sweeper. Rewards reading opponents over raw speed.*

## Identity

Screens buy 5 turns of ~1/3 reduced damage on both sides. That window lets slow, frail sweepers survive hits they normally can't — triggering **Unburden** on Sneasler, **Berserk** on Mega Drampa, and making Mamoswine's 130 Atk impossible to outspeed after Speed Swap. Two physical and one special win condition gives flexible game plans based on what you see in team preview.

## Roster (pick 4 per game)

### Alakazam — Screens + Speed Swap
- **Item:** Focus Sash
- **Ability:** Magic Guard *(prevents passive damage — no weather chip, no hazard damage, survives hits at 1 HP cleanly)*
- **Nature:** Timid (+Speed, -Atk)
- **SP:** Speed / SpAtk
- **Moves:** Light Screen, Reflect, Speed Swap, Protect

### Mamoswine — Physical Speed Swap target
- **Item:** Soft Sand *(20% boost to EQ — no Focus Sash conflict with Alakazam)*
- **Ability:** Thick Fat *(halves Fire and Ice damage — survives hits behind screens)*
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpAtk)
- **SP:** Attack / HP *(Speed investment irrelevant — you're getting Alakazam's 120 Speed via swap)*
- **Moves:** Earthquake, Icicle Crash, Ice Shard, Protect

### Mega Drampa — Berserk special sweeper
- **Item:** Drampanite *(team's Mega slot)*
- **Ability:** Drizzle → **Berserk** (Mega) *(+1 SpAtk automatically when HP drops below 50%)*
- **Nature:** Modest (+SpAtk, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP / SpAtk *(bulk to survive the hit that triggers Berserk; maximize damage output)*
- **Moves:** Hyper Voice, Draco Meteor, Calm Mind, Protect

### Gyarados — Intimidate + Dragon Dance *(Julia's fave)*
- **Item:** Lum Berry *(cures status — especially Thunder Wave which threatens a DD sweeper)*
- **Ability:** Intimidate
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpAtk)
- **SP:** Attack / Speed
- **Moves:** Waterfall, Dragon Dance, Crunch, Protect

> **Mega note:** Mega slot is taken by Drampanite — Gyarados runs base form with Intimidate. Still very good: Intimidate on switch-in + Dragon Dance = independent win condition that doesn't need Speed Swap at all.

### Kingambit — Priority + Fairy answer *(shared with sun team — same moveset)*
- **Item:** Black Glasses
- **Ability:** Defiant
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpAtk)
- **SP:** Attack / Speed
- **Moves:** Kowtow Cleave, Sucker Punch, Iron Head, Protect

### Sneasler — Offensive Fake Out + Unburden sweeper *(shared with sun team — same moveset)*
- **Item:** White Herb
- **Ability:** Unburden *(Speed doubles when item consumed)*
- **Nature:** Jolly (+Speed, -SpAtk)
- **SP:** Attack / Speed
- **Moves:** Fake Out, Close Combat, Dire Claw, Protect

> **The Sneasler combo behind screens:** T1 Alakazam sets screens + Sneasler Fake Outs. T2 Alakazam Speed Swaps + Sneasler Close Combats (White Herb resets the Defense drop, Unburden triggers → 240 Speed). Now Sneasler is the fastest thing alive, clicking Dire Claw (50% status) while screens are still active.

## Lead Options

### Lead A — Unburden setup (standard)
**Alakazam + Sneasler**
- T1: Alakazam screens, Sneasler Fake Outs the biggest threat
- T2: Alakazam Speed Swaps onto Mamoswine (switch in), Sneasler Close Combats → Unburden
- T3: Mamoswine EQ spread at 120 Speed, Sneasler Dire Claw at 240 Speed

### Lead B — Special sweep
**Alakazam + Mega Drampa**
- T1: Alakazam screens, Drampa Protects (let screens land safely)
- T2: Opponent attacks Drampa below 50% HP → Berserk triggers. Or Alakazam Speed Swaps → Drampa fires Hyper Voice / Draco Meteor
- Screens absorb the hit that triggers Berserk

### Lead C — Physical blitz
**Alakazam + Mamoswine**
- T1: Alakazam screens, Mamoswine Protects
- T2: Alakazam Speed Swaps → Mamoswine now 120 Speed
- T3: Mamoswine EQ + Icicle Crash nukes everything

### Lead D — Double Intimidate (vs physical offense)
**Gyarados + Incineroar**
- Don't even bother with screens. Double Intimidate drops opposing Attack by 2 stages. Gyarados Dragon Dances while Incineroar Fake Outs. Independent win con.

## Threats

- **Opposing Sneasler** — Alakazam Psychic is 4x. Mamoswine EQ is 2x. Not a problem.
- **Mega Floette** — Kingambit Iron Head 2x, resists Moonblast. Bring it.
- **Screens getting broken** — Brick Break destroys screens. If you see a Brick Break user in preview, don't over-invest in the screens plan — use Lead D instead.
- **Tyranitar** — Sand Stream cancels nothing (you have no weather), but Rock Slide still threatens Alakazam at 4x if Focus Sash is already spent. Don't keep Alakazam in after screens are up.
