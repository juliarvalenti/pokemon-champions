# Emboar TR Team (Planned)

*Alternate Trick Room comp — mixed physical + special damage with Fire/Fighting coverage. Distinct from [`trick-room-team.md`](trick-room-team.md) (Slowbro variant). This one drops Slowbro and Hatterene for Mega Emboar + Reuniclus to add Fire coverage and a dedicated special wincon.*

## Identity

Same core TR engine (Mimikyu lead, Farigiraf backup, Snorlax Belly Drum) but with a more diverse damage profile:

- **Two physical sweepers** (Snorlax Belly Drum, Conkeldurr punches) handle normal/bulk-breaking jobs
- **Mega Emboar** adds the only Fire STAB on the team + ignores abilities via Mold Breaker
- **Reuniclus** is the dedicated special wincon with Magic Guard immunity to chip damage

The Slowbro variant leans bulk/defense. This variant leans offense/coverage.

## Gameplan

**Default lead: Mimikyu + Snorlax** *(same as Slowbro variant — Mimikyu Disguise protects turn 1 TR setup, Snorlax Protects to scout)*

- **Turn 1:** Mimikyu Trick Rooms (Disguise absorbs any hit). Snorlax Protects.
- **Turn 2:** TR is up. Snorlax Belly Drum, Mimikyu Swords Dances or attacks.
- **Turn 3+:** Bring Emboar, Conkeldurr, or Reuniclus based on matchup.

**Farigiraf is your backup setter, not your lead.** Armor Tail blocks Fake Out priority, so she's the safe mid-game swap-in when Mimikyu falls and TR needs a refresh.

### Damage dealing rules

- **Snorlax** — brute force OHKO via Belly Drum + Body Slam / High Horsepower.
- **Conkeldurr** — Knock Off FIRST to strip Sitrus / Focus Sash / berries, THEN Drain Punch / Ice Punch / Mach Punch. Never brute-force through items.
- **Mega Emboar** — click Flare Blitz and Close Combat for STAB nukes. Mold Breaker lets it hit through Thick Fat (Snorlax), Multiscale (Dragonite), Levitate (Rotom-Wash), Flash Fire, etc. Wild Charge covers Water/Flying threats (rain teams, Gyarados).
- **Reuniclus** — special wincon behind Magic Guard (no burn/poison/hazard chip). Psychic for STAB, Focus Blast for Steel coverage.

### Lead overrides

| If you see... | Lead | Notes |
|---------------|------|-------|
| Aerodactyl / fast Tailwind setters | Mimikyu + Farigiraf | Mimikyu Disguise + TR, Farigiraf Armor Tail blocks Fake Out on the refresh |

| Mega Floette / Fairy setup | Mimikyu + Emboar | Emboar Mold Breaker ignores abilities, Flare Blitz hits Floette... wait — Fairy resists Fire (0.5x). Use **Conkeldurr Knock Off + Drain Punch** instead. |
| Bulky Steel/Grass walls (Aegislash, Sinistcha) | Mimikyu + Emboar | Flare Blitz hits both for 2x, Mold Breaker ignores Sinistcha's Heatproof-style resists if any |

## The 6

### Mimikyu (Lead TR setter)
- **Item:** Mental Herb *(cures Taunt once)*
- **Ability:** Disguise
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpAtk)
- **SP:** HP 2 / Atk 32 / Speed 32 *(NOT slow — Mimikyu plays outside TR)*
- **Moves:** Trick Room, Play Rough, Shadow Sneak, Swords Dance

### Farigiraf (Backup TR setter)
- **Item:** Leftovers *(avoids Sitrus Berry conflict with Snorlax)*
- **Ability:** Armor Tail *(blocks Fake Out and all priority moves)*
- **Nature:** Quiet (+SpAtk, -Speed)
- **SP:** HP 32 / SpAtk 32 / Speed 0
- **Moves:** Trick Room, Hyper Voice, Psychic, Protect

### Snorlax (TR sweeper / win condition)
- **Item:** Sitrus Berry
- **Ability:** Thick Fat *(halves Fire/Ice damage)*
- **Nature:** Brave (+Atk, -Speed)
- **SP:** HP 32 / Atk 32 / Speed 0
- **Moves:** Belly Drum, Body Slam, High Horsepower, Protect

### Conkeldurr (physical hammer + item stripper)
- **Item:** Black Belt *(700 VP — Fighting boost)*
- **Ability:** Iron Fist *(20% punch boost — stacks with Black Belt)*
- **Nature:** Brave (+Atk, -Speed)
- **SP:** HP 32 / Atk 32 / Speed 0
- **Moves:** Drain Punch, Mach Punch, Ice Punch, Knock Off

### Mega Emboar (Fire/Fighting nuker)
- **Item:** Emboarite *(2000 VP — Mega Stone)*
- **Ability:** Blaze → **Mold Breaker** (Mega) *(ignores opponent abilities: Thick Fat, Multiscale, Levitate, Flash Fire, Lightning Rod, etc.)*
- **Nature:** Brave (+Atk, -Speed) *(Emboar is a mixed attacker but physical hits harder with 123 base Atk vs 100 SpA — go physical)*
- **SP:** HP 32 / Atk 32 / Speed 0
- **Moves:** Flare Blitz, Close Combat, Wild Charge, Protect

> **Why Wild Charge over Head Smash?** Wild Charge covers the team's Water weakness — Pelipper, Basculegion, Gyarados, Archaludon all eat Wild Charge. Head Smash hits Flying types but Flare Blitz already covers most Flying (Talonflame, Pidgeot). Electric coverage is the bigger hole.

> **Mold Breaker is the Mega ability — it's why Emboar is the Mega on this team.** It lets you hit through Thick Fat Snorlax mirrors, Multiscale Dragonite at full HP, Levitate Rotom-Wash, Flash Fire Arcanine. No other Mega on your roster gives this specific utility.

### Reuniclus (special wincon)
- **Item:** Twisted Spoon *(700 VP — Psychic boost)*
- **Ability:** Magic Guard *(immune to all indirect damage — burns, poison, hazards, recoil, Life Orb, weather chip)*
- **Nature:** Quiet (+SpAtk, -Speed)
- **SP:** HP 32 / SpAtk 32 / Speed 0
- **Moves:** Psychic, Shadow Ball, Focus Blast, Protect

> **Magic Guard is the hidden value here.** Reuniclus doesn't care about Will-O-Wisp, Toxic, Spikes, sandstorm chip, or any passive damage. She's the mon you bring vs status-heavy teams (Arcanine Will-O-Wisp, Toxapex) and hazard-heavy teams (Glimmora).

## Threats

### 🔴 Trick Room mirrors
Another TR team cancels your TR. Don't re-click TR, your setters waste turns. Instead, out-damage under the *opponent's* TR — Snorlax Belly Drum and Mega Emboar nukes still work in either TR state.

### 🟡 Taunt spam
Taunt locks out Mimikyu and Farigiraf from Trick Room. Mental Herb on Mimikyu cures once. Second Taunt = dead setter. Avoid leading into known Taunt users (Grimmsnarl, Whimsicott) without a backup plan.

### 🟡 Fairy types
Mega Emboar's Fire hits Fairy for 0.5x (Fairy resists Fire). Mega Floette in particular walls Emboar. Use Conkeldurr Knock Off + Iron Fist Ice Punch (no, Ice is neutral on Fairy)... actually use **Reuniclus Psychic or Shadow Ball** for Fairy threats — both hit neutral and Reuniclus is a special attacker that doesn't care about Floette's Defense.

### 🟡 Bulky Water teams (Pelipper + rain core)
Wild Charge on Emboar is the answer. Reuniclus Focus Blast also hits Archaludon for STAB-neutral.
