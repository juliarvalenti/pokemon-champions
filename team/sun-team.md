# Sun Team (Active)

*Mega Charizard Y + Chlorophyll Venusaur core. Fast offensive archetype.*

## Gameplan

**Default lead:** Charizard + Incineroar
- Incineroar Fake Outs a threat → Charizard Megas → Drought sets sun → Heat Wave nukes both opponents

**Follow-up:** Venusaur in the back
- Sun is up → Chlorophyll doubles speed (80 → 160) → Sleep Powder the biggest threat → Sludge Bomb cleanup

**Alt lead vs fast teams:** Whimsicott + Charizard
- Whimsicott sets Tailwind turn 1, Charizard Megas + Heat Waves. Now you're faster AND sun'd.

## Threats to watch

- **Rock Slide spammers** (Aerodactyl, Tyranitar) — Charizard is 4x weak to Rock. Save Charizard for after Rock threats are removed; lead Whimsicott + Incineroar instead.
- **Rain teams** (Pelipper + Basculegion) — rain cancels sun, kills Venusaur's Chlorophyll. Bring Garchomp + Incineroar in this matchup.
- **Fast Fairy setup sweepers** (Mega Floette etc.) — Kingambit is your answer; Steel resists Moonblast/Dazzling Gleam, Iron Head hits Floette for 2x. Bring it in before Floette gets Calm Mind boosts.
