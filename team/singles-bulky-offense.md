# Singles Bulky Offense

*Alternate team for Pokemon Champions **singles** mode (not the default doubles format). Hazard-heavy stall-then-sweep archetype grounded in Smogon OU/UU consensus builds.*

## Status

**Untrained — planned alt team.** Only Tyranitar and Gengar-adjacent trained mons are reusable; most slots need fresh training. VP cost is acceptable (Julia has tokens).

## Identity

**Hazards + trap lock + passive damage + setup sweep.** Lead with a hazard setter, switch to Toxapex who Infestation-traps an opponent and poisons contact attackers via Baneful Bunker, then close games with setup sweepers once the opponent is chipped.

Key difference from doubles-optimized play: **the attackers don't need to break walls directly.** The hazards + poison + burn do the breaking. The offensive slots just finish the job.

## The 6

### 1. Skarmory — Physical wall + hazard setter + phazer
- **Item:** Leftovers
- **Ability:** Sturdy *(guarantees 1 HP survival from full → always gets 1 hazard layer up turn 1)*
- **Nature:** Bold (+Def, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 32 / Def 32 / SpD 2
- **Moves:** **Spikes / Whirlwind / Body Press / Roost**
- **Role:** Lead turn 1 — click Spikes. Stay in for layer 2 if safe. **Whirlwind** is the team's anti-setup answer (phazes out boosted setup sweepers and resets their stages — fills the role Toxapex's Haze used to before we swapped to the Champions trap-set). Body Press scales off Def for damage. Roost for sustain. Immune to all entry hazards as a Flying type.

**Note on Iron Defense:** The Smogon canonical set runs Iron Defense + Body Press for the offensive setup wall play, but with Toxapex losing Haze we need Skarmory to cover anti-setup duty via Whirlwind. The team prioritizes the stall lock + hazard chip plan over the Iron Press damage path. If you find Whirlwind isn't pulling its weight, swap back to Iron Defense.

### 2. Toxapex — Trap-and-chip stall lock
- **Item:** Mental Herb *(Taunt insurance — Taunt is Toxapex's worst enemy; without it you get locked out of Recover and lose. 1-time cure is critical.)*
- **Ability:** Regenerator *(+33% HP on every switch-out — why Toxapex is the best stall mon ever made)*
- **Nature:** Bold (+Def, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 32 / Def 32 / SpD 2
- **Moves:** **Baneful Bunker / Infestation / Toxic Spikes / Recover**
- **Role:** Trap-and-chip lock. Drop Toxic Spikes turn 2 for hazard layer, then Infestation traps an opponent for 4-5 turns of forced 1v1, Baneful Bunker poisons any physical attacker that makes contact, Recover sustains. **Champions Toxapex doesn't run Scald** — the canonical Champions set is Infestation + Baneful Bunker, which is actually MORE oppressive than the mainline Smogon Scald set because the opponent literally can't switch out of the stall lock.

**Why no Haze?** We trade anti-setup utility for the trap lock. Once Infestation is on a target, they can't switch to a setup sweeper, AND the chip damage compounds with Toxic Spikes poison. If the opponent does manage to set up boosts, **Skarmory's Whirlwind** is the team's anti-setup answer — phazing out a boosted mon resets their stages.

### 3. Arcanine — Intimidate pivot + priority + burn spreader
- **Item:** Sitrus Berry
- **Ability:** Intimidate *(drops opponent physical Atk on switch-in — huge defensive support for Skarmory who often eats big physical hits)*
- **Nature:** Adamant (+Atk, -SpA)
- **SP:** HP 2 / Atk 32 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Flare Blitz / Extreme Speed / Will-O-Wisp / Close Combat
- **Role:** Bulky offensive pivot. Will-O-Wisp burns physical attackers (since Toxapex no longer runs Scald, Arcanine is now the team's only burn spreader). Extreme Speed priority revenge-kills setup sweepers that tried to clean late-game. Close Combat for Kingambit / Tyranitar / Steel walls.

### 4. Dragapult — Fast physical sweeper
- **Item:** Dragon Fang *(700 VP — 20% Dragon move boost stacks with Dragon Darts)*
- **Ability:** Clear Body *(prevents stat drops — Intimidate doesn't work on Dragapult, Draco Meteor SpA drop doesn't matter)*
- **Nature:** Jolly (+Speed, -SpA)
- **SP:** HP 2 / Atk 32 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Dragon Darts / Phantom Force / U-turn / Sucker Punch
- **Role:** 142 Speed — tied fastest in the format. Immune to Normal and Fighting, resists half the type chart. Dragon Darts hits twice off 120 Atk. U-turn maintains momentum. Phantom Force bypasses Protect. Sucker Punch priority for revenge kills. **Primary late-game cleaner once hazards have done their work.**

### 5. Tyranitar — Scarf revenge killer *(already trained ✅)*
- **Item:** Choice Scarf
- **Ability:** Sand Stream
- **Nature:** Jolly (+Speed, -SpA)
- **SP:** Attack / Speed
- **Moves:** Rock Slide / Knock Off / Low Kick / Ice Punch
- **Role:** Existing trained set, zero reskilling needed. Scarf Tyranitar is a classic singles revenge killer — outspeeds almost everything at Scarf boost. Knock Off removes opponent items (especially Leftovers from their walls). Low Kick hits Kingambit 4x. Sand Stream chips non-Rock/Ground/Steel types for free. **Caveat:** sand chips 3 of your own 6 mons (Toxapex, Arcanine, Dragapult), so avoid switching TTar in unless you're committing to its turn — don't switch out-and-back.

### 6. Volcarona — Quiver Dance setup sweeper win condition
- **Item:** Charcoal *(700 VP — 20% Fire boost on Heat Wave / Flamethrower)*
- **Ability:** Flame Body *(30% burn on contact — passive support, physical attackers randomly get burned trying to hit Volcarona)*
- **Nature:** Timid (+Speed, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 2 / SpAtk 32 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Quiver Dance / Heat Wave / Bug Buzz / Giga Drain
- **Role:** Primary setup wincon. One Quiver Dance = +1 SpA/SpD/Speed. Two QDs = unstoppable. Heat Wave Fire STAB + Bug Buzz Bug STAB gives strong dual coverage. Giga Drain heals through chip. **Switch in once, set up behind a Skarmory pivot, sweep.** 4x Rock weakness is the main risk — avoid switching into Rock moves and don't switch in repeatedly through opposing Stealth Rock (though Champions barely uses SR — ~1.58% Glimmora usage per Pikalytics).

## Gameplan Cheat Sheet

### Standard lead: Skarmory + bring-in plan

**Turn 1:** Skarmory in. Click **Spikes**. Sturdy guarantees the layer lands even if you eat a super-effective hit.

**Turn 2:**
- If Skarmory is healthy and opponent isn't threatening: click **Spikes** again (layer 2 = 1/6 per switch-in)
- If Skarmory is threatened: switch to **Toxapex**, click **Toxic Spikes**

**Turn 3:**
- If still on Skarmory: third Spikes (max layer = 1/4 per switch-in) or click **Whirlwind** to phaze any setup the opponent attempted
- If on Toxapex: click **Infestation** on the most threatening opposing mon → they're now trapped for 4-5 turns and can't escape the chip stack

**Turn 4+ (stall lock phase):**
- **Toxapex cycles** Recover / Baneful Bunker / Infestation refresh
- Baneful Bunker poisons any contact attacker → free passive damage on top of trapping
- Infestation prevents the trapped opponent from switching to a wall that resists poison/chip
- **Skarmory** sustains with Roost + Leftovers, Whirlwind to reset opponent setup
- **Arcanine** Intimidate refresh + Will-O-Wisp adds more burn pressure
- Opponent can't switch (Infestation traps), can't break Toxapex (Baneful Bunker poisons contact), takes hazard chip + Infestation chip + Toxic poison every turn

**Turn ~10+ (finishing phase):**
- Bring Dragapult or Volcarona in to clean up weakened opponents
- Use Tyranitar Choice Scarf to revenge-kill anything that tries to setup-sweep you first

### Lead overrides

| Opponent lead | Your response |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Physical attacker** (Dragonite, Gyarados, Garchomp) | Lead Arcanine, Intimidate reduces their damage, then pivot to Skarmory for Spikes |
| **Taunt user** (Whimsicott, Grimmsnarl, Gengar) | Lead Skarmory anyway — Spikes layer 1 before Taunt lands. Mental Herb on Toxapex saves it later. |
| **Setup sweeper** (Volcarona, Calm Mind Clefable, DD Dragonite) | Lead Tyranitar (Scarf) — revenge kill before they set up |
| **Mega Floette / Fairy setup** | Lead Skarmory — Steel resists Fairy, Body Press hits Fairy for neutral. Backup: **Skarmory Whirlwind** to phaze the boosted Floette out (resets the Calm Mind stages on switch). |
| **Opposing stall** | Lead Dragapult — Phantom Force + U-turn pressure, avoid getting locked into the stall mirror |

## Threats this team dies to

- **Taunt spam without Mental Herb up** — Toxapex Taunted = dead team
- **Strong Fighting STAB** (Conkeldurr, Sneasler) — 4x on Tyranitar, neutral on most others but Close Combat hurts
- **Stealth Rock + repeated Volcarona switches** — rare in Champions but punishing when present. Don't over-rely on Volcarona.
- **Magic Guard users** (Clefable, Reuniclus) — immune to poison/burn/hazard chip, bypasses the entire stall-lock game plan. **Skarmory Whirlwind** can phaze them out of CM boosts, but they'll just come back in with the boosts erased and try again. Best plan: revenge-kill with Tyranitar Scarf + Conkeldurr Drain Punch / Mimikyu Shadow Sneak before they get too far into setup.

Before committing the VP to this team, verify in-game:
- **Spikes** is available as a TM/trainable move in Champions
- **Toxic Spikes** is available on Toxapex
- **Infestation** and **Baneful Bunker** are available on Toxapex (top usage Champions Pex moves per Pikalytics — should be there)
- **Whirlwind** is available on Skarmory (alternative to phaze)
- **Mental Herb** is in the shop (CLAUDE.md confirms 1000 VP)
- **Dragon Fang** and **Charcoal** type boosters are in the shop (CLAUDE.md confirms 700 VP each)
- **Dragapult** and **Volcarona** have the moves listed in their learnsets (Champions may restrict moves from mainline)

**Confirmed unavailable on Champions Toxapex:** Scald (per in-game observation 2026-04-14). The original Smogon Scald-based set won't work — use the Infestation + Baneful Bunker trap-lock build instead.

Other fallbacks:
- No Spikes? Skarmory runs Stealth Rock instead (note: Glimmora is the only mon currently using SR in Champions per Pikalytics, so it may be available)
- No Toxic Spikes? Toxapex runs Toxic (single-target) + maybe Knock Off in the 4th slot
- No Whirlwind on Skarmory? Run Body Press + Iron Defense instead and accept losing the anti-setup option (rely on Tyranitar Scarf to revenge setup sweepers)
- Mental Herb missing? Fall back to Leftovers on Toxapex (Skarmory swaps to Shell Bell or type-resist berry)
