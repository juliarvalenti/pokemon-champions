## Status

## Honest framing

- **Zero Psychic Terrain teams in top-20 of the last 10 Champions tournaments.** Archetype is ladder-proven (u/No_Advice_7485 hit Master Ball with Prankster Meowstic + Mega Alakazam) but not tournament-validated.
- **Indeedee is NOT in Champions** — no auto-setter exists. Psychic Terrain must be set via the move.
- **Mega Delphox has Levitate post-Mega** → cannot benefit from Psychic Terrain boost. Don't build toward it.
- **Mega Alakazam is the star Expanding Force abuser** — 175 SpA / 150 Spe post-Mega, grounded (unlike Delphox).
- **Espeon is the backup setter + Calm Mind sweeper + Magic Bounce insurance**, not the main wincon.

## Gameplan

**Default lead: Meowstic + Mega Alakazam.**
- T1: Meowstic Prankster-priority Psychic Terrain OR Fake Out to disable threat. Alakazam Megas + Protects (scouts opponent's lead).
- T2: PT is up. Alakazam clicks Expanding Force (80 BP → 120 BP under PT, spread damage in doubles). Meowstic supports with Helping Hand or Light Screen.
- T3+: Alakazam sweeps under terrain. Meowstic pivots or re-applies PT if it expired.

## Planned 6

**Mega slot:** Alakazam (Alakazite, 2000 VP — required purchase).

### Meowstic-M (primary PT setter)
- **Item:** Mental Herb *(1000 VP — cures Taunt. Fast Prankster Taunts from Whimsicott/Sneasler leads will brick Meowstic otherwise)*
- **Ability:** Prankster *(Psychic Terrain at +1 priority = always goes up T1)*
- **Nature:** Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 32 / Def 2 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Psychic Terrain, Fake Out, Helping Hand, Light Screen
- *Note: **Meowstic-MALE has Prankster**. Meowstic-F has Competitive. Make sure you recruit/recognize the male form.*

### Alakazam (Mega) — Expanding Force nuke
- **Item:** Alakazite *(2000 VP Mega Stone)*
- **Ability:** Inner Focus → **Trace** (post-Mega — copies opponent's ability)
- **Nature:** Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 2 / SpAtk 32 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Expanding Force, Dazzling Gleam, Shadow Ball, Protect
- *Expanding Force under PT = 120 BP spread. Dazzling Gleam hits Dark (Kingambit/H-Zoroark). Shadow Ball for Ghost/Psychic coverage. Protect for Multiscale-style survival.*

- **Nature:** Timid (+Spe, -Atk)
- **SP:** HP 2 / SpAtk 32 / Speed 32
- **Moves:** Psychic Terrain, Expanding Force, Dazzling Gleam, Calm Mind
- *Role: insurance. If Meowstic dies or gets Taunted, Espeon re-sets PT. Calm Mind lets her pivot into a win con under screens. No Protect — Focus Sash is the "Protect."*

## Bring-4 logic

**Always bring:** Meowstic + Mega Alakazam (the PT core).

**Flex 2 from Espeon / Incineroar / Hatterene / Whimsicott depending on matchup:**

| Matchup | Flex 2 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Rain** (Pelipper/Bascu/Archaludon) | Incineroar + Whimsicott | Intimidate + Tailwind + Moonblast KO Bascu |
| **Mega Floette / Fairy spam** | Incineroar + Espeon | Incin **Taunt** shuts off Floette CM; Espeon Dazzling Gleam mirror |
| **Trick Room opponent** | Hatterene + Espeon | Double Magic Bounce reflects their Trick Room back — *they* end up in TR while you benefit |
| **Sand (TTar/Exca)** | Incineroar + Whimsicott | Intimidate + Moonblast chip + Tailwind outspeed Exca |
| **Kingambit / Dark spam** | Incineroar + Whimsicott | Avoid leading Psychics into Kowtow Cleave — Fake Out + burn + Moonblast |
| **Scovillain glue** | Incineroar + Whimsicott | Incin Taunt blocks Rage Powder/Leech Seed; Moonblast hits Primarina |

## Known weaknesses

1. **🔴 Dark-type pressure** — Kingambit Kowtow Cleave, H-Zoroark, Sneasler Dire Claw all delete the Psychic trio. Incineroar + Whimsicott are the only non-Psychic answers. **Biggest structural hole.**
2. **🟡 Bug coverage** (U-turn, Lunge, Pollen Puff) hits all three Psychics — but Bug isn't heavily played in the current meta.
3. **🟡 Gholdengo** if it's meta — Steel/Ghost walls Psychic STAB, Make It Rain shreds. No Ground move on team.
4. **🟡 Fast Taunt leads** (Sneasler Fake Out + ally Taunt, Prankster Whimsicott) — Meowstic Mental Herb helps once, then team falls apart.
5. **🟡 PT decay** — PT only lasts 5 turns, no Psychic Surge auto-renewal. Espeon's PT move is the backup.
