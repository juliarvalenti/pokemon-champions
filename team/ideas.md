## "Burn Bull" — Bulky Offense (needs no new pulls!)

**Concept:** Use Gengar's Will-O-Wisp on your own Conkeldurr to activate Guts (+50% Atk, ignores burn's attack drop). Doubles allows targeting your own ally. Works *outside* Trick Room because Gengar (110 spe) reliably moves before Conkeldurr (45 spe) in normal speed order, so the burn lands and Conkeldurr swings same turn.

**Why this is cool:** Champions doesn't have Flame Orb, so this is the only way to get Guts Conkeldurr. Iron Fist is solid but Guts is ~25% stronger.

**Why not in TR:** Under Trick Room speed reverses — Conkeldurr would attack BEFORE Gengar burns him. Doesn't work in TR.

**Possible 6:**
- **Gengar** (lead, Will-O-Wisp turn 1 to burn ally)
- **Conkeldurr** (lead, swings at +50% turn 1 with Guts)
- **Whimsicott** (Tailwind speed control)
- **Incineroar** (Fake Out + Intimidate)
- + 2 more (TBD — maybe Garchomp for physical pressure, Aegislash for defensive utility)

**Status:** All pieces owned. Just need to train Conkeldurr with **Guts ability** (not Iron Fist) and pick a Black Belt item. Then play test.

## Mega Meganium Solo Sun (validated by Extreme Speed tournament winner!)

**Status: VALIDATED — corrected from earlier "low priority" take. Won the Extreme Speed Pokémon Champions tournament (107 players, 2026-04-13) on team Punihina.**

**Concept:** Mega Meganium's Mega ability is **Mega Sol** — its moves act as if in harsh sun *for Meganium only*, without setting weather on the field. The key trick: **Solar Beam becomes a 1-turn move** (no charge needed) without requiring actual sun. Meganium becomes a 1-turn-Solar-Beam spammer that doesn't need any team sun support.

**Why I was wrong earlier:** I previously said "Mega Sol doesn't set real sun, so it's worse than Charizard Y." That's true for *team-wide* sun abuse, but it MISSED the point: Meganium's value isn't enabling teammates, it's that **Solar Beam is normally 2 turns, and Meganium fixes that for itself.** It's a unique single-mon trick, not a team weather strategy.

**Reference winning build (Punihina, Extreme Speed):**
- **Meganium** (Meganiumite, Overgrow → Mega Sol)
- Moves: Solar Beam / Dazzling Gleam / Weather Ball / Protect
- Plays alongside Sneasler / Tyranitar / Wash Rotom / Excadrill / Dragapult

**Status:** Niche but real. **Spend 2000 VP on Meganiumite** if you want a fun secondary special attacker. Lower priority than Sneasler / Excadrill / Aerodactyl pulls but ahead of pure cope picks like Beedrill.
