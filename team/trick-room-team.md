# Trick Room Team (Alt Comp)

*Slow-mode TR team. Mechanically opposite of the sun team.*

## Status

**Mostly trained.** Original 5 (Hatterene / Snorlax / Reuniclus / Conkeldurr / Mimikyu) + Sylveon were trained earlier. **Reuniclus → Slowbro swap in progress (2026-04-14)** — Slowbro brings a Mega slot, Iron Press damage path, and Slack Off sustain that Reuniclus didn't. Reuniclus stays trained on bench as backup vs hazard-heavy opponents.

## Gameplan

**Default lead: Mimikyu + Snorlax** *(updated 2026-04-13 after losing games leading Hatterene first — she's too slow to reliably set TR before Fake Out / chip damage kills her)*

- **Turn 1:** Mimikyu Trick Rooms. Disguise absorbs whatever hits her (Fake Out, Knock Off, anything). Snorlax Protects to scout opponent moves.
- **Turn 2:** TR is up. Snorlax Belly Drum (Sitrus heals back), Mimikyu Swords Dances or Play Roughs the biggest threat.
- **Turn 3+:** Snorlax sweeps, Mimikyu cleans with priority Shadow Sneak post-TR if needed.

**Hatterene is your BACKUP TR setter, not your lead.** She comes in mid-game (turn 3-4) when:
- Mimikyu falls and TR needs to be refreshed
- You have a free turn to swap her in safely
- Magic Bounce is needed to reflect Taunt/Will-O-Wisp from a specific opponent

### Why Mimikyu first, not Hatterene

Hatterene at 29 base speed is the slowest mon on most fields. Before TR is up, she goes LAST every turn — meaning Fake Out spam (Incineroar is on 48% of teams) and chip damage kill her before she gets to click Trick Room. Mimikyu's Disguise gives a guaranteed turn of setup regardless of what hits her, which is exactly what you need turn 1.

### Damage dealing rules

**Snorlax = brute force OHKO.** Belly Drum first, then Body Slam / High Horsepower. At +6 Attack, Snorlax breaks through Sitrus Berries and most Focus Sashes in one shot.

**Conkeldurr = setup KO.** Knock Off FIRST to remove Sitrus / Focus Sash / type berries, THEN Drain Punch / Ice Punch / Mach Punch on the next turn. Don't try to brute-force — the opponent's item will eat your big hit. Trade one turn of Knock Off for guaranteed kills the turn after.

**Hatterene = special wall breaker.** Expanding Force / Dazzling Gleam — these don't have item removal so you have to chip through berries. Hit the same target multiple turns in a row.

**Slowbro = setup-then-Body-Press-everything.** Iron Defense compounds with Mega Slowbro's 180 Def. After 1 Iron Defense click you're at ~270 Def, which makes Body Press a 135 BP physical attack scaled off your defensive stat. After 2 Iron Defenses, you're one-shotting walls. Slack Off between to stay at full HP — Mega Slowbro is essentially impossible to kill from the physical side once set up.

## Bench backup

- **Reuniclus** *(trained)* — Magic Guard makes her nearly immune to indirect damage (poison, hazards, burn, Life Orb recoil). Swap her in for Slowbro when facing:
  - **Hazard-stacked teams** (Glimmora, Spikes setters) — Magic Guard's full-immunity beats Slowbro's bulk-and-recover loop
  - **Charizard Y sun teams** — Slowbro is 2x Grass weak and gets melted by Solar Beam / Energy Ball; Reuniclus's Magic Guard at least immunizes her against burn/Toxic chip from Toxapex-ish backup mons, and Focus Blast hits Charizard neutrally

## Known bad matchups (acknowledge and play tight)

These are matchups where the team has structural weaknesses — note them in team preview and pick your battles:

### 🔴 Charizard Y + Venusaur sun (~15-20% of teams)
- **Slowbro is a liability** — 2x weak to Grass (Solar Beam, Energy Ball, Giga Drain), 80 SpD melts to Heat Wave
- Snorlax can Belly Drum + Body Slam Charizard if TR is up, but it's not a clean win
- **Best plan:** Lead Mimikyu + Conkeldurr, set TR turn 1, click Drain Punch on Charizard / Ice Punch on Venusaur. Bring Hatterene as a 2nd threat (4x SE on Venusaur via Psychic? no — Venusaur is Grass/Poison, Psychic is 2x on Poison only = 2x). Slowbro stays in the back unless you absolutely need her bulk against a specific physical threat.

### 🔴 Sneasler-led teams
- **Snorlax dies to Sneasler outside of TR** — Fake Out + Close Combat chain OHKOs through 65 Def + 2x Fighting weakness
- **Best plan:** Mimikyu + Hatterene lead. Mimikyu's Disguise blocks Fake Out, sets TR turn 1. Hatterene's Psychic (Psychic 2x on Fighting * 2x on Poison = 4x) deletes Sneasler. Snorlax comes in TR-safe on turn 3.
- **Don't lead Snorlax against Sneasler ever** — even with Sitrus Berry and Belly Drum plan, Sneasler outpaces the setup turn.
