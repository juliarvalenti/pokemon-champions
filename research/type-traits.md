# Type Traits (Intrinsic Type Abilities)

*Source: in-game Pokemon Champions type-trait reference screen (captured 2026-04-14).*

**These apply to every Pokemon of the listed type regardless of species or ability.** They're silent baseline abilities the game doesn't always highlight but that matter for team building, move selection, and threat assessment.

## The Traits

| Type | Intrinsic Trait |
|------|----------------|
| **Fire** | Cannot be burned |
| **Dark** | Immune to Prankster-boosted status moves |
| **Electric** | Cannot be paralyzed |
| **Fairy** | Immune to Dragon-type moves |
| **Flying** | Immune to Ground-type moves |
| **Ghost** | Immune to Normal and Fighting moves. **Immune to trapping effects.** |
| **Normal** | Immune to Ghost-type moves |
| **Ground** | Immune to Electric-type moves |
| **Steel** | Immune to Poison-type moves |
| **Grass** | **Immune to Powder moves** (Spore, Sleep Powder, Rage Powder, Stun Spore, etc.) |
| **Ice** | Cannot be frozen. **Defense boosted in Snow weather.** |
| **Rock** | **Special Defense boosted in Sandstorm weather.** |
| **Poison** | **Absorbs Toxic Spikes on entry** (removes them from your side) |

## Non-obvious implications for team building

Several of these go beyond the basic type chart and meaningfully change competitive interactions:

### 🛡️ Grass immunity to Powder moves
- **Venusaur's Sleep Powder can't put opposing Grass types to sleep.** So a Grass-type opponent (Sinistcha, Venusaur mirror, Meganium, Gourgeist, Decidueye, etc.) completely counters Venusaur's utility move.
- **Rage Powder redirection fails on Grass types too.** Opposing Sinistcha's Rage Powder can't redirect away from a Grass attacker's spread move.
- This is part of why **Sinistcha-on-Sinistcha matchups** are so awkward — neither can redirect the other's attacks.

### 🛡️ Dark immunity to Prankster status
- **Whimsicott's Prankster Taunt / Encore DOES NOT WORK against Dark types.** Kingambit, Tyranitar, Sneasler, Hydreigon, Incineroar, Gengar... wait Gengar is Ghost/Poison. Sneasler is Fighting/Poison. Let me be careful.
- **Dark types shut down Whimsicott Prankster:** Tyranitar, Kingambit, Hydreigon, Zoroark, Sharpedo, Greninja, Weavile.
- This is why Kingambit is such an effective counter to Whimsicott leads — Prankster Taunt fails, and Kingambit Sucker Punch / Kowtow Cleave deletes it.
- **Julia's Whimsicott:** needs to know this when picking leads. Don't click Prankster Taunt on Tyranitar / Kingambit / Incineroar — it fails and wastes the turn.

### 🛡️ Ghost immunity to trapping
- **Shadow Tag Mega Gengar CANNOT trap Ghost type opponents.** So if the opponent has Sinistcha, Gengar mirror, Dragapult, Aegislash, Froslass, Golurk, Mimikyu, or any Ghost type, Mega Gengar's trap plan fails against those specific mons.
- Perish Song still works, but they can switch out freely — no trap pressure.
- **Implication for the Mega Gengar trap team idea:** lead Gengar against non-Ghost opponents. Against Ghost-heavy teams, Perish Song is a wasted slot.

### 🛡️ Poison absorbs Toxic Spikes
- **Toxapex on entry removes opponent Toxic Spikes from YOUR side.** If you're playing a stall team and the opponent tries to Toxic Spike you, a single Toxapex switch-in wipes their hazards.
- This is a defensive bonus for Poison-type walls in general: **Toxapex, Muk, Gengar (Ghost/Poison), Beedrill, Venusaur (Grass/Poison), Sneasler (Fighting/Poison)** all remove Toxic Spikes on entry.
- **Grass types in opposing Toxic Spikes:** Venusaur gets poisoned from Toxic Spikes normally (it's not Flying) BUT because it's Poison, it absorbs them. Venusaur is actually a weirdly strong Toxic Spikes counter because of the dual-type.

### ❄️ Ice Def boost in Snow
- Mega Froslass sets Snow Warning (weather change). Under Snow, **all Ice types get +50% Defense**. This makes Froslass into a surprisingly bulky physical wall while active.
- Implication: Mega Froslass teams want **multiple Ice types** to maximize the Snow Def buff (e.g., Weavile, Mamoswine, Ninetales-Alola). Julia doesn't have a snow team but this is worth knowing for matchup reads.

### 🗿 Rock SpDef boost in Sand
- Tyranitar's own Sand Stream gives **every Rock type on the field +50% SpDef**. Tyranitar itself effectively becomes a special wall under its own sand.
- **Garchomp is NOT rock-type,** so Garchomp on your sand team doesn't benefit from this. But Tyranitar, Excadrill (Ground/Steel — NO, not rock, doesn't get it), Aerodactyl (Rock/Flying — YES, gets the boost), and Aggron (Steel/Rock — YES) all benefit.
- Implication for **sand team building**: Aerodactyl + Tyranitar synergizes doubly — Aerodactyl gets both Tailwind utility AND the SpDef boost in TTar's sand.

### 🔥 Fire can't be burned
- Will-O-Wisp fails against Fire types. So your Arcanine / Incineroar / Charizard / Volcarona / Ceruledge / Torkoal can't get burned.
- More importantly: **opposing Fire types are immune to the burn attrition plan.** In your singles bulky offense team, Toxapex Scald's burn chance does nothing against Incineroar or Arcanine. Against Fire teams, lean on Toxic Spikes + Toxic instead.

### ⚡ Electric can't be paralyzed
- Thunder Wave fails against Electric types. Your own Wash Rotom is Thunder Wave-immune, which makes it a safe switch-in vs paralysis spammers.

### 🧊 Ice can't be frozen
- Ice Beam 10% freeze chance can't touch Ice types. Doesn't come up often but worth knowing for Froslass / Weavile / Mamoswine matchups.

## How to use this file

When recommending moves or team interactions, **check this file first** for silent type-based immunities that might invalidate the plan. Examples:

- *"Lead Whimsicott Prankster Taunt against Tyranitar"* → **NO**, Dark is Prankster-immune, the Taunt fails
- *"Sleep Powder the opposing Sinistcha"* → **NO**, Grass is Powder-immune
- *"Mega Gengar trap the opposing Dragapult"* → **NO**, Ghost is trap-immune
- *"Wash Rotom gets paralyzed by Thunder Wave"* → **NO**, Electric is paralysis-immune
- *"Burn Arcanine with Will-O-Wisp"* → **NO**, Fire can't be burned
