# Team Archetypes

*Durable team-building templates. These describe the SHAPE of viable teams in Champions VGC.*

Every doubles team needs answers to four questions:
1. **Win condition** — who deals the killing blow? (sweeper, setup attacker, spread move spam)
2. **Speed control** — Tailwind, Trick Room, or priority moves?
3. **Defensive utility** — Intimidate, redirection, Fake Out, screens?
4. **Type coverage** — can you handle the top meta threats?

Each archetype below answers these in a different way.

---

## Sun Offense

**Identity:** Fast offense built around weather. Sun setter Megas turn 1, sweepers abuse the weather.

**Setters:** Mega Charizard Y (Drought via Mega), Torkoal (Drought, slow — for TR sun)

**Abusers:**
- Chlorophyll mons (Venusaur, Lilligant) — speed doubles in sun
- Solar Beam users — 1-turn move in sun
- Fire types (Charizard, Talonflame) — Fire moves boosted 50%

**Key items:** Charizardite Y (2000 VP), Twisted Spoon / Fairy Feather as Life Orb subs

**Weak to:**
- **Rain teams** (Pelipper) — rain cancels sun, kills Chlorophyll
- **Tyranitar** (Sand Stream) — sand cancels sun
- **Stealth Rock** in singles, but Champions has limited hazards

**Sample core:** Charizard Y + Venusaur + Incineroar + Whimsicott + Garchomp + flex slot

## Sand Offense

**Identity:** Tyranitar's Sand Stream sets weather, Sand Rush mons get doubled speed in sand.

**Setters:** Tyranitar (Sand Stream)

**Abusers:**
- Excadrill (Sand Rush — speed doubles in sand). 93.7% of Excadrill teams run Tyranitar — highest co-occurrence in the format.
- Rock-types get a SpDef boost in sand

**Key items:** Tyranitarite (2000 VP) optional, Excadrite optional, Focus Sash on Excadrill is most common

**Weak to:**
- **Sun teams** (Charizard Y) — sun overrides sand if Charizard switches in last
- **Mega Floette** — outdamages most sand mons
- **Bulky Water types** (Pelipper, Toxapex)

**Sample core:** Tyranitar + Excadrill + Incineroar + Sinistcha + Whimsicott + flex

## Rain Offense

**Identity:** Pelipper's Drizzle sets rain, Swift Swim mons get doubled speed, Water moves are 50% stronger.

**Setters:** Pelipper (Drizzle), Politoed (Drizzle)

**Abusers:**
- Basculegion (Adaptability + Wave Crash — STAB Water move with rain boost)
- Archaludon (Stamina, takes hits in rain)
- Other Swift Swim mons

**Key items:** Mystic Water (700 VP) for Water boost stacking with rain

**Weak to:**
- **Sun and sand teams** (override rain)
- **Grass types** (Venusaur, Sinistcha) — resist Water
- **Electric types** (Rotom forms, Manectric)

**Sample core:** Pelipper + Basculegion + Archaludon + Sinistcha + Incineroar + flex

## Trick Room

**Identity:** Reverse the speed clock. Slow Pokemon move first inside Trick Room. Built around 3-turn explosive damage windows.

**Setters:** Hatterene (Magic Bounce blocks Taunt), Reuniclus (Magic Guard, very bulky), Mimikyu (fast TR setter, Disguise insurance), Farigiraf (top TR meta pick)

**Abusers:** Slow heavy hitters
- Snorlax (Belly Drum sweeper)
- Conkeldurr (Iron Fist or Guts physical hammer)
- Crabominable (Iron Fist via Mega)
- Slow Sylveon (Pixilate Hyper Voice spread)
- Slow Mega Mawile / Camerupt / etc.

**Key items:** Mental Herb (Taunt cure), Sitrus Berry (Belly Drum recovery), Twisted Spoon (Life Orb sub)

**Weak to:**
- **Taunt before TR sets** (mitigated by Magic Bounce on Hatterene)
- **Long games** — TR only lasts 5 turns. If you can't win in that window, you lose.
- **Mons with priority moves** (still go before TR mons)

**Sample core:** Hatterene + Snorlax + Reuniclus + Conkeldurr + Mimikyu + Sylveon (slow Pixilate)

## Pure Stall

**Identity:** Wear opponents down with hazards, status, and recovery. Win by attrition. Wants 30+ turn games.

**Setters:** Skarmory (Spikes, Roost, Body Press), Toxapex (Toxic, Recover, Scald)

**Walls:**
- Skarmory (physical wall, 140 Def, Steel/Flying)
- Toxapex (special wall, Regenerator, Poison/Water)
- Corviknight (mixed wall, Pressure, Bulk Up)
- Clefable (Magic Guard, Calm Mind)
- Cresselia (if available — bulky support)

**Win conditions:**
- Calm Mind setup (Clefable, Cresselia)
- Body Press scaling off Defense
- Toxic + stall

**Key items:** Leftovers, Rocky Helmet *(not in Champions — substitute with Sitrus or type berries)*, Heavy-Duty Boots *(not in Champions)*

**Weak to:**
- **Setup sweepers** that can boost past your damage cap (Mega Floette CM, Mimikyu SD)
- **Taunt** users (Whimsicott Prankster Taunt)
- **Knock Off** spam (removes your Leftovers)

**Champions caveat:** Champions has a reduced item pool. Many stall staples (Heavy-Duty Boots, Rocky Helmet, Eject Button) don't exist. Stall is therefore weaker in Champions than in mainline VGC. Work in progress.

**Sample core:** Skarmory + Toxapex + Corviknight + Clefable + Aegislash + flex (need most of these to make stall work)

## Hyper Offense Tailwind

**Identity:** Set Tailwind turn 1, sweep with fast attackers. Short games, no defensive game plan.

**Setters:** Whimsicott (Prankster — priority Tailwind), Aerodactyl (fast natural Tailwind), Talonflame (Gale Wings priority)

**Abusers:** Anything fast and offensive
- Garchomp, Mega Hawlucha, Weavile, Sneasler, Mega Beedrill (145 spe)
- Charizard, Dragapult

**Key items:** Focus Sash on the setter (guarantees Tailwind goes up), type-boosting items on attackers

**Weak to:**
- **Trick Room teams** — Tailwind makes you fast, TR makes their slow mons faster than yours
- **Priority spam** (Extreme Speed, Sucker Punch, Bullet Punch) — bypasses your speed advantage
- **Bulky teams** that can survive 4 turns of pressure until Tailwind expires

**Sample core:** Whimsicott + Garchomp + Mega Hawlucha + Incineroar + Weavile + flex

## Bulky Offense (no specific weather/room)

**Identity:** Mid-speed, mid-bulk. No weather setup, no Trick Room. Win through Intimidate cycling, status, and trades.

**Setters:** No speed control beyond priority moves and Incineroar's Fake Out

**Win conditions:** Aegislash setup, Mega Hawlucha No Guard sweeps, Garchomp brute force

**Key items:** Sitrus Berry, Lum Berry, type-boosting items

**Weak to:**
- Dedicated weather/room teams that out-speed or out-power you
- Setup sweepers without Intimidate / Fake Out chip pressure

**Sample core:** Incineroar + Aegislash + Garchomp + Whimsicott + Hawlucha + flex

This is the "default" bulky balance team. Decent into everything, great into nothing. Good for learning the game; usually replaced by a more committed archetype as you build collection.
