# Core Pairs

*High-co-occurrence teammate pairs from Pikalytics usage data. Helps explain WHY certain mons want certain partners.*

A "core pair" is two Pokemon that show up together far more often than chance would predict. When usage data shows a 60%+ teammate rate, that's a hard signal that one mon needs the other to function.

## Top Cores

### Excadrill ↔ Tyranitar — 93.7%
**The strongest co-occurrence in the entire format.** Almost every Excadrill team runs Tyranitar.
- **Why:** Tyranitar's Sand Stream activates Excadrill's Sand Rush ability, which doubles Excadrill's speed in sand. 88 base speed → ~176 effective. Excadrill becomes the fastest physical attacker on the field while also hitting like a truck (135 Atk).
- **Implication:** If you pull one without the other, you've activated half of an S-tier core. Pulling the second is high priority.

### Venusaur ↔ Charizard — 87.4%
- **Why:** Mega Charizard Y sets Drought (sun). Venusaur with Chlorophyll has its speed doubled in sun (80 → 160). Together they form the iconic sun offense core.
- **Implication:** These two are a package deal. Pulling either one without the other significantly reduces its value. Don't Mega Venusaur — Mega Venusaur loses Chlorophyll.

### Basculegion ↔ Pelipper — 65.5%
- **Why:** Pelipper's Drizzle sets rain. Basculegion with Adaptability + STAB Wave Crash hits absurdly hard in rain. Swift Swim variants double speed.
- **Implication:** Basculegion without Pelipper is significantly weaker. If you pull Basculegion (or have it on your bench), Pelipper becomes a high-priority recruit target.

### Basculegion ↔ Archaludon — 68.3%
- **Why:** Both abuse rain. Archaludon's Stamina ability makes it tankier as it takes hits, and Pelipper rain enables both attackers simultaneously.
- **Implication:** Rain teams want all three (Pelipper + Basculegion + Archaludon). Pulling any one of them activates the archetype potential.

### Mega Charizard Y ↔ Incineroar — 58.1%
- **Why:** Incineroar provides Fake Out (helps Charizard set up Mega safely), Intimidate (defensive support), and Parting Shot (pivot). The best support in the game enables the strongest sun setter.
- **Implication:** This is true for most top sweepers — Incineroar is on ~half of all teams. If you have a setup sweeper, you almost always want Incineroar.

## How to use this

When suggesting a Pokemon to pull or train, **check what cores it activates or completes**. A mon with no core is much weaker than a mon that activates a 60%+ pair.

When building a team around a core mon, **the other half of the pair is usually the best second mon to add**. Don't think in isolation — think in pairs.

When seeing an opponent's team in preview, **identify the core pair first**. That tells you the team's win condition, which tells you what to lead and what to save for the back.
